# Acme Corp Remote Work & Home Office Policy 2026

## 1. Eligibility & Scope
All full-time and part-time employees are eligible for hybrid or fully remote work arrangements, subject to formal written approval from their department head.

## 2. Core Collaboration Hours
To facilitate effective cross-functional teamwork, all remote team members must be online and available on Slack, email, and Google Meet during core collaboration hours:
- **Core Hours:** 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST), Monday through Friday.

## 3. Equipment & Home Office Stipend
Acme Corp provides an annual home office equipment stipend of **$1,200 per employee**, distributed via reimbursement.
- **Eligible Items:** Ergonomic office chairs, standing desks, high-speed fiber internet subscription, external 4K monitors, and noise-cancelling headsets.
- **Submission Window:** Expenses must be submitted in the Expensify portal within 60 days of purchase.

## 4. International Remote Work Guidelines
Employees desiring to work outside of their primary tax residence country may do so under the following conditions:
- **Advance Notice:** Requests must be submitted at least **4 weeks in advance** to the People Operations team.
- **Maximum Duration:** Limited to a maximum of **30 calendar days per calendar year** to comply with international corporate tax and labor law compliance.
- **Security:** Employees must connect exclusively through the corporate WireGuard VPN and avoid public unencrypted Wi-Fi networks.
